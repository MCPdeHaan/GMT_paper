# R10a. Table IV observability and bracket transport

The reference is R09b (`473ff6fff3392952f13874c017469dec54fee0cf`), with standard role-specific CIT rates, verified parent-year-only UTPR and the continuous behavioural response. This is an isolated diagnostic.

## Prespecified comparison

- **LEGACY** reproduces the R09b pooled selector, bilateral exact selector and both transport paths.
- **COMPLETE_DIRECT_ONLY** requires an observed non-negative lower profit, an observed non-negative upper profit, a positive observed denominator, numerically valid published profit components, and an observed non-negative tax for each published lower component with positive profit. A lower component with explicit zero profit can contribute zero if its tax is zero or missing. An absent lower or upper side at the measurement grain cannot be certified as zero. The rule operates on the published 15%-compatible partition. It cannot establish completeness of the underlying reporter population. Retain the existing fallback hierarchy and BSPI anchors.
- **COMPLETE_DIRECT_AND_BSPI** applies the same rule to direct observations and to each anchor year before pooling the remaining 2019–2020 numerators and denominators. A single admissible year may anchor both earlier years. Use the existing fallback when no valid anchor remains.
- **BSPI_ONLY** retains direct measurement and screens anchors, to identify interaction with direct screening.
- **PARTIAL_INFORMATION_BOUNDS** reports conditional ranges for observed profit support. It provides neither a fitted point nor a fiscal scenario. A separate, stronger support check requires both sides for each contributing parent and exposes sensitivity to unknown bracket attendance.

All comparisons retain the R09b Table I estimates, percentile ranks, caps, policy maps, SBIE inputs and applied behavioural coefficient. The diagnostic does not use fiscal outcomes to decide which component is observable.

## What the source identifies

OECD Data Explorer calls this flow Table IV (`DF_CBCRIV`). The reporting template calls it Table I.5 and the 2026 statistical release calls it Table 5. It bins the **jurisdictional subgroup** of an MNE by its accrued-current-income-tax-to-profit ratio. The corresponding group-rate table instead sorts by the ratio of summed group taxes to summed group profits. Within a published schedule the bins are mutually exclusive. Non-positive profit has priority for its own category. A profitable subgroup with negative accrued current tax belongs in a separate category in the modern schedule. The model's positive-profit denominator is specifically for the selected non-negative-accrual partition. Current accrued tax measures current tax expense or credit and excludes deferred tax. Cash receipts can differ in timing and sign. Neither numerator identifies GloBE covered taxes. [OECD Table IV metadata](https://data-explorer.oecd.org/vis?df%5Bag%5D=OECD.CTP.TPS&df%5Bds%5D=dsDisseminateFinalDMZ&df%5Bid%5D=DSD_CBCR%40DF_CBCRIV) · [OECD 2026 reporting chapter](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/corporate-tax-statistics-2026_73af6222-en/full-report/country-by-country-reporting-statistics_06824b57.html) · [OECD Action 13 guidance, §4](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-sub-issues/cbcr/guidance-on-the-implementation-of-country-by-country-reporting-beps-action-13.pdf).

The recent schedule uses `[0%,15%)` and `[15%,∞)`. The historical instructions allowed different levels of disclosure, including 0–10, 10–20, 20–30 and 30+, a broad ≥20 or ≥10 category, and under the least granular schedule a <10 category which includes negative accrued tax. A rate interval spanning 15% cannot identify each side of the floor. Historical 20–30 and 30+ reports provide upper-side evidence but do not resolve the missing 15–20 portion of any 10–20 report. The R09b kernel includes upper categories recognised by its code. Neither the audit nor the production model should interpret their presence as proof that every positive-profit subgroup in the jurisdiction is represented. Compare the actual schedule by parent, affiliate and year in `reporting_profiles.csv`. [OECD FY2023 guidance, §§1.1.3 and 2.3.5–6](https://survey.oecd.org/upload/surveys/251718/files/OECD%20CbCR%20Guidance.pdf) · [OECD FY2021 reporting instructions, Table I.5](https://survey.oecd.org/upload/surveys/253426/files/OECD%20CbCR%20Database%20Guidelines%202021.pdf).

Use `Q` plus a blank value as affirmative evidence of suppression. An `A` blank is unclassified in this extract. An omitted measure in an existing bracket, an entirely absent bracket and an observed numeric zero are separate events. The OECD permits coarser geographical disaggregation where necessary. Its reporting instructions do not give an absent country-specific row a general structural-zero interpretation. Conversely, failing the two-sided screen does not prove a nonzero hidden quantity. The project originally treated one-sided upper reporting as credible zero-low-tax evidence. A verified observation count of zero or a reporter-specific exhaustive tabulation could support that inference for particular observations. [OECD FY2023 guidance, §§1.1.3 and 2.3.1](https://survey.oecd.org/upload/surveys/251718/files/OECD%20CbCR%20Guidance.pdf) · [OECD statistical disclaimer](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-sub-issues/corporate-taxation/anonymised-and-aggregated-cbcr-statistics-disclaimer.pdf) · [Historical design decision](../archive/code_logbook.md#L265).

## Partial information

For each reported positive lower component with observed profit, missing current accrued tax in a correctly classified non-negative lower bracket lies in `[0, 0.15 L_i)`. With `L` the observed combined lower profit, `M` the portion with missing tax and `A` observed tax, the closure of the conditional gap interval is `[0.15 − (A + 0.15 M)/L, 0.15 − A/L]`. The bracket-scale top-up interval is obtained by multiplying by `L`. At `L=0` the contribution is zero and the conditional gap is undefined. Before computing bounds the audit excludes incompatible observed tax amounts or signs. Binding the gap is possible even when an upper component is unavailable. The share is point identified on a valid observed denominator. With known lower profit and unknown non-negative upper profit its interval is `[0,L/(L+U_observed)]` for a positive total. Where lower profit itself is unknown, the audit records the model's generic share range and leaves the monetary gap bounds unavailable. These statements condition on the published 15%-compatible population, without a bound on other unreported parent groups. They do not hold for a separate negative-accrued bracket, cash taxes or a mixed historical interval crossing 15%.

For the three US-parent cases the observed-bracket top-up widths are US$1.79m, US$5.11m and US$16.13m respectively, with zero as the lower limit. A model sensitivity on corrected Table I profit can multiply bounds on the rate by a fixed Table I denominator. SBIE can then scale by a held fixed non-negative shield factor. Those are model-conditional intervals on the projection, with no inference for revised behavioural flows, sector scaling or final collector receipts. The audit does not impose a Table I accounting total as a hard bound on differently covered Table IV subgroups.

## R10b decision

Adopt the **direct-and-anchor complete published partition** rule as the leading point specification. It avoids both verified blank-to-zero defects and repeated transport of a shape whose two sides cannot be established. Keep the original pooled result as a reproducible comparison. A deliberately narrower sensitivity should isolate screening of incomplete **published measures** from the evidence requirement for categorical attendance. A one-sided bracket can still inform an observed numerator, published profit amount or bound. Permit it as an exact two-sided point only with reporter- and vintage-specific structural-zero evidence. The large Macao, Mauritius and Bermuda movements justify showing this assumption explicitly. Requiring full bilateral attendance for every contributing parent is too strong for the pooled estimator's target when no independent partition for the full parent population exists.

Carry reporter, host, year, bracket, measure and source vintage through ingestion, with row presence, measure presence, original status, observed zero, finite validity, lower/upper support counts, unexplained omissions and known compatible numerator and denominator components. Retain the exact rejection reason, estimator tier, relevant support population, anchor-year set and source years used for transport. Keep pooling for the published host population and matched bilateral support distinct. Separate completeness of profit/share from support for the tax/gap. Preserve conditional bounds as separate diagnostic fields. A share-and-fitted-gap hybrid would combine distributions weighted by different populations and needs independent evidence before use.

Use direct L3 in 2019–2023 where sufficiently supported, Table I percentile fits where available in 2021–2023, screened BSPI where an admissible 2019 or 2020 partition supports earlier years, then host average and unmeasured. Preserve the L4 priority for contemporaneous bilateral exact, bilateral percentile fit, optional anchored precision and L2. Keep the pair-BSPI sensitivity distinct. Preserve R09b rate roles, cap, reconciliation, legal allocation, applied response and the S06–S08/S10 boundaries.

R10b must recompute validation overlap and support-specific calibration, both SBIE constructions, Stage 05 estimates and the applied specification, all 32 core scenarios and the wider accounting design, policy deviations, the budget comparisons and fiscal extensions. Recalibrate and run the Stage 07a measurement-error and profit-correction studies and Stage 11 joint draws from the new intermediate inputs. The Stage 07a [cache gate](../../code/07a_uncertainty.R#L1104) cross-checks input and output hashes and must not reuse a reference cache across changed support. Report post-reform numerical effects and uncertainty separately, revise §§4.1–4.2 and 5, Appendix B and all dependent exhibits, and publish a versioned new freeze only after an isolated leading replay and preserved legacy replay pass. No decision on hybrid estimation, undisclosed subgroup populations, revised uncertainty laws or rank-mean conflict is made here.
