## Orthogonal effects and what their shares mean

For each observational unit, let N = 2^K and define h(S, x) as the product of ±1 codes for the factors in nonempty set S. The coefficient is the mean of h(S, x) times revenue. It is half the signed high-minus-low difference for a main effect. Each term has sum of squares N × coefficient². The total is the sum of centred squared scenario outcomes in that unit. For pooled analyses, **square within the unit, then pool the sums of squares**. Shapley splits each interaction's sum of squares equally between its member factors. These are equally weighted policy counterfactuals, not a model of policy probabilities or causal variance.

Stage 05 divides pooled term sums by the sum over effects. Stage 07 divides by the pooled centred outcome SS. Complete orthogonality makes the denominators equal, apart from evaluation order. The six-factor stress and accounting calculators use matrix columns for observational units, enforce orthogonality and check closure. The accounting output's named `factorial_variance_usd_bn_sq` equals SS divided by N. In the pooled case this is the **sum** of country-year population variances, without an extra division by the number of country-years. The Stage 08 ladder orthonormalises the Helmert contrasts in the declared level order, then tensors with the binary bases. Three Helmert components belong to one ETR factor when allocating interaction credit. The ladder tolerates neither a missing finite outcome nor an unbalanced cell. Stage 09 stress and ladder both assume positive total variance. Stage 11 accounting emits undefined shares when variation is zero. Stage 07 terms are undefined at zero total SS, but its Shapley summary turns all-undefined inputs into zeros via `sum(na.rm = TRUE)`. Preserve those distinctions in compatibility views or label them explicitly.

The 0, 2, 3, 9 fixture has mean 3.5, population variance 11.25 and coefficients 2, 2.5 and 1. The variance terms are 4, 6.25 and 1. Shapley shares are 40% and 60%. Replicating the four cells over inactive factors leaves those values intact. A pure three-way interaction splits its entire contribution by thirds.

With two equally likely outcomes of 0 and 10, each country's variance is 25. A reverse profile in a second country has covariance −25 with the first. Total variance is 25 + 25 + 2(−25) = 0. Reverse both profiles in a second year and the result also holds within each country across years. The sum of the four country-year variances is 100. Annual EU27 sums are equal across scenarios, their average is also constant, and cumulative national and EU27 variances are zero. Add an independent second-factor effect of 4 in one country and the pooled Shapley split is 50/54 versus 4/54, while the cumulative split is 0 versus 1. The cancellation is an **aggregation effect**. Generally each term gains cross-products when one sums contrast coefficients before squaring. The same logic applies to averages with the relevant scaling. [Actual pooled, annual and cumulative contrasts](r13a/aggregation.csv).

Missing scenario cells are never a licence to apply a balanced contrast. Stage 07's inner wrapper checks 32 rows/IDs. Its upstream check confirms IDs against factor text and counts. The bare helper coerces values and has no support guard, and Stage 05's wrapper assumes a prebuilt complete design. The diagnostic validates numeric binary domains, exact bit positions, matching keys, balance and finite outcomes independently. Under unsupported designs, the current wrappers can otherwise accept an invalid factor value or project nonorthogonal cells. Treat this as an exposed input-contract weakness. The frozen input grids meet the strict checks.

## Ladder support and attribution

L1 applies the host average and its floor to the corrected positive base. L2 applies an available bilateral mean as a point mass. L3 fits or observes a host distribution using two-sided direct Table IV where supported, statutory-capped Table I percentile fits, shape transport, or an average fallback. Anchored L4 prioritises the exact bilateral Table IV partition and exact Table I fit, then transports precision from the pair, host-year or host median, centred at the bilateral mean. Strict L4 uses exact support or a bilateral mean and avoids that transport. The separately labelled strict BSPI sensitivity imputes an early partition only on observed anchor pairs. An exact Table IV label means eligible published bracket inputs, not observation of a continuous distribution. Lack of measured share/gap stays visible in the support audit even where the numerical amount becomes zero. [Support by source tier and year](r13a/ladder_support.csv) · [Ranges of the six individual 16-case levels](r13a/ladder_totals.csv).

Stage 08 L1 versus L3 is the complete 32-case core on matching support. A single L3 row family conditions on x1=1 and includes 16 cases. The L3-only range is US$367.670–673.405bn, compared with US$196.277–673.405bn across the full core. The 64-row L1/L2/L4/L3 ladder comparison uses four levels on one factor. It neither switches accounting nor adds a binary sixth factor. Accounting strict L4 and accounting L3 each include an average branch as well as their own distributional branch. The top-up rate cap and Table IV observability guard continue to apply. S10's mean/rank choice remains open.
