## S08. Monte Carlo coverage and variance interpretation

**Defensible conditional approximation and an uncovered cache-invalidation risk.** There are three uncertainty exercises, with different estimands. Profit-only draws in 07a hold ETR measurement and strategic inputs fixed (95% intervals). Legacy Stage 1 error calibration resamples paired forecast errors and propagates through the core design. [11:531–605](../../code/11_policy_game_and_variance_extensions.R#L531), [11:738–870](../../code/11_policy_game_and_variance_extensions.R#L738) use two independent samples of three input blocks (paired ETR residuals within a draw, one normal linear coefficient, bounded-logit posterior reconciliation), common draws across scenarios, equal scenario weights and 500 draws. The resulting 90% preferred interval and variance split are conditional on that selection and dependence structure. Zero or modest variance on the residual-calibrated post-2019 component partly reflects the direct Table IV assignment and which cells get perturbed. Shared measurement, profit denominators, validation transport and estimation support can induce dependence across blocks, hosts and years. Accounting/legal uncertainty lies outside the parameter simulation. The 64-case factorial treats accounting as a design switch, with a different aggregation estimand for cumulative receipts than pooled country-year sums of squares. Neither decomposition identifies a probability distribution over policy choices.

**Evidence and next test (R16).** The frozen diagnostic has 2,576 rows and no negative reported raw or scaled orthogonal components. In the code, finite-sample cube interaction estimates can be signed and are rescaled to add to the separately estimated parameter variance. The total-variance identity and point parity check arithmetic, with no check on simulation Monte Carlo error or validity of the draw law. Add a hand-computable input-block fixture and check seeds, cross-scenario pairing, perturbed support, numerical closure, stability over draw number/seed and plausible dependence. Different draw law or support needs separate scientific review. Replace or scope the 07a verification cache so stale generator logic cannot pass a change review. In Section 5.2 and Appendix D, qualify the 98.29% scenario share, ratios and Shapley shares as conditional on this design. [Paper §5.2](../../writing/paper_v2.tex#L320).
