# Handbook for developing and writing the thesis for the MSc Economic Policy at Utrecht University

## General information
**Topic**: Global Minimum Tax
**Thesis coordinator Economic Policy**: dr. Thomas van Huizen
**Thesis supervisor**: [dr. Peter Gerbrands](https://www.uu.nl/staff/PGerbrands)

## Objectives 

### Goals of thesis course
- assess advanced economic theory
- apply modern economic theories, in combination with other academic fields
- work with economic models
- conduct high-level scientific research independently
- academic attitude for solving complex and intertwined economic problems
- select and use relevant research methods (theory, empirical, or combination)
- position thesis within existing knowledge
- write advanced research report in English

### Personal goals for thesis


## Requirements research proposal (period 3)
- 5 ECTS, write research proposal, research gap, research questions, literature review and collect data, plan for empirical data anlysis, in end present and discuss proposal, 10% final grade, between 3000-5000 words
- discuss fraft between 6-14 April, deadline 15 April 

## Requirements final thesis (period 4)
- 10 ECTS, data collection, analysis data, drafting and improving thesis, 90% grade, length: 8000-12000 words (excluding executive summary, reference list, appendices)
- discuss fraft between 15-23 June
- deadline 26 June final thesis 

## Factors taken into account in grading process

- Societal relevance
- Scientific relevance
- Methodology
- Research approach and implementation
- Interpretation of findings
- General presentation
- Conducting research in independent and self-responsible wat
