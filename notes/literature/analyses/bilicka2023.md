# Bilicka, Devereux & Güçeri (2023) — "Tax-Avoidance Networks and the Push for a 'Historic' Global Tax Reform"
**Tax Policy and the Economy, 37, 57–108**

# Detailed notes


**P. 57:**
Use of IP in tax avoidance. Income from IP taxed in location where income is received, therefore use tax havens for IP ownership.
Cayman Islands and Liechtenstein have more patents per citizen than countries like China and US.
5% of patents in European market sold in tax havens and 20% of global cross-border patent transactions have buyers from tax havens.

**P. 59:**
Orbis IP datasets, authors in past have used PATSTAT and US IPO, but Orbis has broader coverage than EU and US. Link subsidiary locations to parent company location: firms potentially affected with GMT.
Only show small piece of larger puzzle on IP tax avoidance since cost-sharing agreements between parent company and tax-haven affiliate can be used to make tax-haven affiliate beneficial owner with rights over significant portion of royalty income of IP.

**P. 60: Findings:**

* Broad global patenting activity: large share of patenting and patent ownership in countries with strong innovation ecosystem (China, Japan, US, South Korea, large EU MS)
* Large share of patenting by Pillar 2 firms
* Patenting mostly done by top quantile of turnover size distribution
* Disproportionate share of patenting takes place in tax havens with no/little R&D and investment hubs where strong local innovation ecosystems
* Affiliates located in Europe have higher portion of patent applications that end up being held in tax havens (5% of applications held in tax havens compared to 2% of firms located in Asia)
* Tax haven affiliates play more significant role in patents that have higher predicted value and more forward citations

**P. 68: Datasets used:**

* Orbis IP: patents back to 1800
* Patent header (133m), of which 122m filed after 1980
* Patent publication number, country apply/publish, date application/publication/granting, number of citations


* Orbis Lookup data: bridge between application and applicants, 86m pairs
* Universe of patent transactions 1980-2020, 83m patent transactions related to 8m patents
* Patent value between 2010-2020 (estimation)


* General Orbis: Pillar 2 firms in 2019, collect data on financial and ownership structure
