## The Global Minimum Tax — and why nobody agrees on the money

In 2021, 137 countries agreed on a **15% minimum tax** on large multinationals.
The EU implemented it. Governments are already budgeting on it.

**The problem:** revenue estimates range from almost nothing to hundreds of billions of euros — and nobody has explained *why* they differ.

The answer lies in three modelling choices that every study must make:

1. **How do you measure which profits are actually low-taxed?**
2. **How much of the base do substance exemptions carve out?**
3. **How much profit do multinationals stop shifting once the tax kicks in?**

My research question is:

> *How sensitive are EU minimum-tax revenue estimates to these choices — and which one drives the uncertainty?*

I answer this by estimating profit-shifting behaviour on seven years of OECD data, then running all 32 combinations of assumptions through a single revenue model.
